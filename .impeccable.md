## Design Context

### Users
The owner coordinates local engineering projects through native Claude and Codex.
They want to describe goals in Chinese, let Opus clarify/specify work, and confirm
execution rather than operate a developer console or manually compose check JSON.

### Brand Personality
Calm, direct, trustworthy. Show what is proposed, running, and independently
verified without making the user interpret transport receipts or raw events.

### Aesthetic Direction
User explicitly selected: light, simple, like Claude. A warm paper-like workspace,
quiet conversation typography and a restrained earth accent. Avoid a neon/dark
admin dashboard, repeated metric cards and persistent raw JSON walls.

### Design Principles
- Conversation is the primary surface; project and evidence use progressive disclosure.
- Plan first, explicit confirmation second, native execution third.
- Preserve approved checks; conversational prose never supplies completion truth.
- Make uncertain results and failures visible; never silently resend a request.
- Support keyboard, Chinese IME, reduced motion and narrow-screen drawers.
